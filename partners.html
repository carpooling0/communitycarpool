For your members

Why they Join

The pitch to your community is short, and it is the same one that works on a flyer or in a newsletter.

  • Connect with people from your own community
  • Less stress, with fewer cars at the gate
  • Reduce your carbon footprint together
  • Build real connections with the people nearby
The problem

It is not unwillingness. It is coordination.

In fast-growing cities a single new school or office nearby can turn a 15 minute commute into 35. Most students and staff still travel one car, one person. Not because people are unwilling to share, but because there has never been a simple, private way to organise it.

  • Increased stress
  • Ever increasing commute times
  • Higher emissions

“People are willing to carpool. They just need a safe and simple way to do it.”

How a partnership works

Live in a day, nothing for you to maintain

We build and run the whole thing. You share one link with your community.

1

We create your page

A dedicated landing page, a unique link and a QR code, branded for your organisation.

2

You share it

Send it through your newsletter, parent groups, staff email or noticeboard. We can supply a printed flyer and poster.

3

Members register a journey

They enter their home area and destination. No apartment or villa numbers are ever needed.

4

Smart matching runs

Members are matched only with other members of your community who travel a similar route.

5

Both sides opt in

Contact details are revealed only when both people express interest. All communication happens over email.

6

They carpool

Arrangements are made directly between members, at their own discretion.
